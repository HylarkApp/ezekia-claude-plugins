# Daily Briefing

Check the calendar for tomorrow and prepare for each meeting: cross-reference
attendees against Ezekia contacts and the projects they're linked to,
surfacing anything relevant to the call.

Check Slack (DMs, mentions, and channels) for anything time-sensitive or
decision-worthy that needs attention.

Check the inbox for emails needing a response.

Send the briefing as a Slack message to the user, formatted for Slack, with
meetings separated by a line. Keep each section tight — this replaces a
5-minute skim, not a full report.
